Experience

Data Science Intern, Dato Solutions

Research & Development · Asunción · Aug–Oct 2026

Paraguay’s public customs data stopped naming importers in late 2024. I built the model that fills that gap, so the company’s product can show who imports what again.

  • Compared four approaches on more than 26 million customs records. A text-similarity model (TF-IDF with nearest neighbours) more than doubled accuracy over the old hand-written rules.
  • It only publishes a prediction when it is confident: 60% of shipments get one, and 86.5% of those are right. Totals were checked against Central Bank statistics.
  • Built the batch pipeline on Amazon Redshift, resumable after interruptions, with data transfers 3.6× faster.
  • Wrote an automated test suite for the company’s AI query assistant and fixed the calculation errors it found.

Nightly, a private sleep companion

iPhone and Apple Watch app · 2026 · in progress

I wanted to keep track of my sleep, so I built an app for iPhone and Apple Watch. The iPhone listens through the night and keeps a clip only when there’s sound, transcribing any sleep talk on the phone. The Watch adds movement, heart rate, estimated sleep stages and a smart alarm. A coach that runs on the device answers questions about your own nights. Nothing leaves your devices.
